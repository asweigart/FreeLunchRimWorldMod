# Free Lunch (FreeMealRimWorldMod)

A RimWorld mod that adds a zero-cost packaged survival meal bill to vanilla stoves and campfires. Intentionally overpowered — useful if you want to remove food production as a factor in your game.

Published on the Steam Workshop as **"Free Lunch"** ([Workshop item 3451996585](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3451996585)).

## What it does
Adds a new bill, **"Cook free packaged survival meal"**, to:
- Fueled Stove
- Electric Stove
- Campfire

Each meal costs zero resources and 70 ticks of work (about one in-game second of cooking animation at base CookSpeed).

## Supported RimWorld versions
- 1.4
- 1.5
- 1.6

## Known limitations
- Only vanilla cooking workbenches gain the recipe. Modded stoves — for example Vanilla Cooking Expanded, Dub's Bad Hygiene kitchen units, and VFE-Production ovens — are not supported.
- Cooks gain no Cooking XP from this recipe.
